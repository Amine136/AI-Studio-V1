# Vibecraft Platform Policy

This document is the working MVP policy draft for Vibecraft. It defines acceptable use, account rules, credit usage rules, and the basic enforcement posture for the platform.

## Purpose

Vibecraft is a creative AI platform for generating images and text content. The service is intended for legitimate creative, marketing, and business use. The platform is not intended for abuse, fraud, harassment, or harmful content generation.

## Account Rules

- Each person should maintain only one primary account.
- Users must provide truthful account information.
- Users may not create multiple accounts to bypass usage caps, redeem more credits, or avoid moderation.
- Accounts may not be sold, shared, rented, or transferred without explicit written approval from Vibecraft.
- Vibecraft may limit, suspend, or terminate accounts involved in spam, fraud, or repeated abuse.

## Credit And Redemption Rules

- Credits may only be used through normal Vibecraft product flows.
- Credit codes may not be abused, brute-forced, or redeemed through multi-account farming.
- No account may redeem more than 4 credit codes in 1 day.
- No account may redeem more than 10 credit codes in 7 days.
- Vibecraft may disable codes or suspend redemption access when suspicious usage is detected.

## Usage Limits

The platform may enforce technical or commercial usage limits in order to protect service quality and provider costs.

Current MVP policy:

- In the first 24 hours after account creation, an account may consume up to 1 credit total.
- After the first 24 hours, an account may consume up to 5 credits in a rolling 24-hour period.
- Smart workflow analysis fees count toward account usage limits.
- Generated content usage also counts toward account usage limits.

These values may change over time as the product evolves.

## Acceptable Use

Users may use Vibecraft for lawful creative and business purposes, including:

- marketing visuals
- product images
- campaign drafts
- captions and copywriting
- concept exploration

## Prohibited Use

Users may not use Vibecraft to generate, request, distribute, or promote:

- explicit sexual or pornographic content
- exploitative, abusive, or non-consensual sexual content
- child sexual content or any sexualized content involving minors
- violent extremism, terrorism, or criminal instruction
- fraud, impersonation, scams, phishing, or deception
- hateful, discriminatory, or targeted harassment content
- illegal goods, illegal services, or illegal activity
- malware, credential theft, prompt injection attacks, or platform abuse tooling

Users also may not use the platform to test provider abuse paths, bypass moderation, or reverse engineer protected system behavior.

## Enforcement

Vibecraft may take any of the following actions when abuse is detected:

- reject a request
- rate limit a request
- block uploads or generation temporarily
- apply credit restrictions
- disable redemption access
- suspend the account
- permanently terminate the account in severe cases

Enforcement may be automated, manual, or both.

## Admin And Security Monitoring

Vibecraft may retain operational security records for:

- credit charges
- suspicious burst usage
- moderation actions
- code creation and code disabling
- abuse-related events

These records may be used for alerting, investigation, anti-fraud controls, and future automation.

## Future Verification Policy

For later versions of the product, Vibecraft may require stronger verification for higher limits or commercial usage, including:

- phone verification
- stronger identity or business validation
- verified billing status

Verified users may receive expanded usage access compared with unverified users.

## Operator Notes

This document is a product policy draft, not a final legal terms-of-service document. Before public scale, it should be split into:

- Terms of Use
